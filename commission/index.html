Original Music for Film, Series & Games

Yuriy Leontiev composes original scores for films, documentaries, television series and games. Directors, broadcasters, independent filmmakers and game developers can commission a score that combines orchestral writing with ambient textures and electronics. You can also ask about licensing an existing track.
